Garden of the Groves & Freeport City Tour

  • Participants age 3+ — $69
  • Children age 0–2 — free (still recorded)

4 hours. Meeting point is approximately a 5–10 minute walk from the cruise ship pier. Exact meeting instructions are provided after confirmation / on your e-ticket.

Narrated vehicle tour with Freeport / recovery-area context, Garden of the Groves, and Port Lucaya Marketplace or an approximately 45-minute Lucaya Beach option, plus Royal Palm Way / Cooper's Castle narrative context. Easy activity level; surfaces may include paved, gravel, wooden plank, packed dirt, and a few steps. Wheelchair accessibility is not stated. Stay with the group for included return transport; staying behind means arranging your own return to the pier.
