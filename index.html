Grand Bahama orientation

Freeport is the call name — Grand Bahama is the island

Cruise passengers usually arrive at Lucayan Harbour (the Freeport cruise terminal). Port Lucaya is a separate tourist marketplace and marina area. Lucayan National Park and Gold Rock Beach sit farther east. Confusing those names is the fastest way to build an unrealistic day.

Freeport shore excursions FAQ

Is Freeport a dock port?›

Most standard Freeport cruise calls dock alongside at Lucayan Harbour (also called the Freeport cruise terminal). That is different from Port Lucaya, the tourist marketplace. Some Carnival Corporation sailings use Celebration Key — a separate private pier with different logistics.

What should first-time visitors do in Freeport?›

Choose one primary plan that fits your usable hours: a Garden of the Groves / city-style outing, a Lucaya-area beach day, or — on a fuller window — Lucayan National Park or a dedicated snorkel excursion. Avoid stacking east-island nature, beach, and city shopping on a short call.

Is Port Lucaya the cruise pier?›

No. Port Lucaya is a tourist marketplace and marina area. You typically need a taxi or organised transfer from the cruise terminal. Do not assume a walkable tourist strip at the pier.

What is Lucayan National Park versus Garden of the Groves?›

Garden of the Groves is a botanical garden outing often paired with Freeport / Port Lucaya sightseeing. Lucayan National Park sits farther east and includes cave areas and Gold Rock Beach access — treat it as a dedicated half-day-style commitment, not a quick add-on.
